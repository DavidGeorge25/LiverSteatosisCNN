FIRST THING IT ASKS
  Where to keep your results file. Choose somewhere you can find it --
  Documents is fine. From then on, every answer is written to that file the
  moment you make it. You never have to remember to save.

  (Chrome or Edge give you that automatic file. Safari and Firefox do not
  support it and the page will say so -- there your work is kept in the
  browser and you press "Save a copy" to get the file out. Chrome is the
  easier ride.)

WHAT WE ARE ASKING YOU TO JUDGE
  Two things, and they are separate questions.

  1. In each FIELD, circle every ballooned hepatocyte you can see.
  2. After the last field of a SECTION, give that section a NASH-CRN
     ballooning grade: 0 none, 1 few, 2 many.

  The fields are a uniform random sample of the section -- not selected by
  any algorithm -- so what you see is representative of the whole section.

WHAT COUNTS AS BALLOONED
  Enlarged relative to the neighbouring hepatocytes, rounded rather than
  polygonal, with rarefied or flocculent cytoplasm, often a hyperchromatic
  nucleus, and frequently near a terminal hepatic venule. The panel on the
  right shows the surrounding tissue, because "enlarged" is a comparison and
  the neighbours are what it is against.

  Please use UNSURE freely. Ambiguity is information here; a forced call is
  noise, and we would rather have an honest "cannot tell" than a guess.

THE TOOL
  click            place a circle
  drag             move it; drag the corner handle to resize it to the cell
  red X            remove that circle
  Y                record the field with the circles you have drawn
  N                no ballooned cells in this field
  U                unsure / cannot judge this image
  left arrow       go back to the previous field
  scroll           zoom; 0 resets the view
  Delete           remove the selected circle

  Pressing Y with no circles drawn asks once whether you meant to, then
  records it if you press Y again.

STOPPING AND RESUMING
  Close the tab whenever you want, mid-section is fine. To carry on, open the
  same link and type the same initials -- it resumes at the first field you
  have not done and reconnects to the same results file.

WHEN YOU ARE DONE
  Email the results file back. Please also say if anything felt wrong --
  a field you could not judge, a tool that got in the way, a question that
  was the wrong question. That is as useful to us as the answers.

IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
  Stop and email David George, davidg1091620@gmail.com. Nothing is lost by
  stopping -- your file is written after every answer -- and a problem found
  in the first ten minutes is much cheaper than one found at the end.
